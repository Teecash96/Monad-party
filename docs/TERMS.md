# Terms of service draft

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Proof of Play is experimental software. Entry does not guarantee a prize. Blockchain transactions can fail, cost gas, or become final.

Users must control the wallet and X account they connect. Automated follower abuse, identity resale, Sybil behavior, and attempts to manipulate eligibility can lead to exclusion where permitted by the published rules.

Prize claims must occur within 30 days after the draw. Unclaimed prizes can roll into a later epoch.

The sponsor can pause new user actions during a security incident. Published draw results and valid claims cannot be changed by the web application.

This draft needs legal review, the sponsor identity, governing law, eligibility countries, tax terms, sanctions terms, age limits, and dispute rules before production use.
